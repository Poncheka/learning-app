Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Pliny is an app that makes short audio stories about the things you are curious about. It is made by Nigh High LLC, a California limited liability company ("Nigh High", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the Pliny app, the website at itspliny.com and our emails (together, "Pliny"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect what we need to make and play your stories, we do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Email address. When you join the waitlist or sign in. We use it to send sign-in links and, if you joined the waitlist, to tell you when your invite is ready.
  • Your name and profile picture. The first name you give us, and a profile picture if you add one.
  • Your interests. The topics you choose or type in, such as "urban legends" or "space".
  • Your feedback. Stories you like, turn down, swipe away or queue.

Information created when you use Pliny

  • Listening activity. Which stories you start, the length you choose, how far you get and when.
  • Stories you share. When you share a story, we create a link to a public page for it. The page shows the story and the first name of the person who shared it.
  • Referrals. If you join the waitlist from a shared link, we note who shared it, so they can move up the list.
  • Technical information. Like any internet service, our servers receive your IP address and basic device and request details, and keep them in short-lived logs to run and secure the service. The app also checks for updates, which tells our update provider which version you have.

We do not collect your contacts, location, microphone or photos, other than a profile picture you choose to upload.

How we use it

  • To run Pliny: sign you in, suggest and make stories, play them, and keep your library.
  • To personalize: your interests and feedback shape which stories we suggest to you.
  • To share stories you choose to share, and to run the waitlist and referrals.
  • To keep Pliny working and safe: fixing problems, preventing abuse and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • To contact you about your account, your invite, or changes to these terms. We do not send marketing emails without your agreement.
  • To count how stories do across all listeners, such as how often a story is liked or finished, so better stories are offered first. These counts do not identify you.

How stories are made

Pliny's stories are researched from public sources such as Wikipedia, then written and narrated by AI. To suggest and write stories, we send story subjects, research notes and your interests to our AI providers. We do not send them your email address or your name. Story scripts are sent to our voice provider to be read aloud. Stories are not about you, and are shared with other listeners once made.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who run parts of Pliny for us under contract: Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), OpenAI (story writing), ElevenLabs (narration), Resend (email), Expo (app updates), Apple (app distribution) and GitHub (website hosting). The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • People you share with. A shared story's page is public to anyone with the link, and shows your first name.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or Nigh High.
  • A change of ownership. If Nigh High is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may transfer, still covered by this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your email, name, picture, interests and listening history within 30 days, apart from what we must keep by law. Stories themselves are not personal information and stay available to other listeners. Waitlist emails are kept until you get an invite or ask us to remove you. Server logs are kept for a short time.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your name, picture and interests in the app at any time.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it and your account, by emailing info@itspliny.com.
  • You can stop waitlist emails by replying to any of them or emailing us.

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to have it deleted or corrected, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in that law, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. To make a request, email info@itspliny.com. We will confirm your request comes from you, usually by email, before acting on it. You may use an authorized agent.

If you live elsewhere, you may have similar rights under your local law, and you can use the same email to exercise them.

Security

Your data is stored with our providers in the United States, protected by access controls and encrypted in transit. Sign-in uses one-time email links, so we never hold a password for you. No service is perfectly secure, and we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information, as the law requires.

Children

Pliny is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
